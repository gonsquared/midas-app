# N. Short title of the decision

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** who agreed to this

## Context

What problem or force makes a decision necessary? State the constraints and the facts, not the
solution.

## Decision

What was decided, in one or two plain sentences. Start with "We will".

## Alternatives rejected

- **Option:** why it was not chosen.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what must now be true (follow-up work, risks, limits).
